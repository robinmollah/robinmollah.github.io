About

I am Robin Mollah, a software engineer focused on building systems that survive real constraints: limited hardware, high traffic, growing data volume, and production pressure.

My work sits across backend engineering, cloud infrastructure, data systems, and applied AI. I care most about the part where architecture decisions meet operational reality: performance, reliability, cost, maintainability, and systems that keep working when conditions are not ideal.

What I work on

  • Backend platforms and service architecture
  • Cloud infrastructure and deployment systems
  • Data processing and analytics pipelines
  • Performance optimization and reliability work
  • Practical AI integration

How I work

I prefer clear system boundaries, simple interfaces, and designs that can be defended technically. I am most useful when the work is ambiguous but the constraints are real: stabilizing systems under load, reducing operational complexity, and turning vague goals into concrete plans.
