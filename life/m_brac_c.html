Chapter 03c / University Project
BRACU Bot
Chatbot · Regex & Rule Engine · University
What It Was
A chatbot built entirely on regex patterns and handcrafted rules — no machine learning, no pretrained models. Just logic, pattern matching, and a good map of how students ask questions.
Who Used It
BRAC University students. Built for the university itself — routing common queries about courses, schedules, and campus information. It worked because the rules were written by someone who understood the problem.
What It Taught
That you don't need AI to solve a well-scoped problem. Precision beats complexity when the problem space is understood.
